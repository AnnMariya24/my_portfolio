§ 06 — How We Work

The 4-Step Redesign Process

A structured, reliable workflow designed to deliver measurable results on time.

PHASE 01

UX Audit & Discovery

We evaluate your existing app, pinpoint friction points, analyze cognitive overload, and define clear redesign objectives.

PHASE 02

Figma Redesign

Crafting modern high-fidelity screens, cohesive design tokens, typography scales, and interactive prototypes for your review.

PHASE 03

Flutter Engineering

Writing maintainable Dart widgets, integrating state management (GetX/Bloc), setting up API connections, and maintaining clean code.

PHASE 04

60 FPS QA & Handover

Rigorous cross-platform testing on real Android and iOS devices, frame rate profiling, animation tuning, and store-ready delivery.
